# ORVYN: 12\-Month Go\-To\-Market \(GTM\) Strategy

## Phase 1: The Closed Beta & "High\-Signal" Waitlist \(Months 1–3\)

- __Objective:__ Establish core performance metrics and iron out bugs with an initial batch of 5,000 highly active power users\.
- __Tactic:__ Launch an invite\-only web teaser explaining the concept of a "Personal Digital Home" to clear out the tech jargon early\. Give digital\-creator influencers early access to configure custom AI templates\.

## Phase 2: The Nexus Launch & Utility Acquisition \(Months 4–6\)

- __Objective:__ Scale to 50,000 users by focusing on the app's utility as a unified aggregator rather than promoting it as a brand\-new social network\.
- __Tactic:__ Run targeted marketing materials highlighting a single, universal pain point: *"Stop playing app\-hopscotch just to reply to your friends\."*

## Phase 3: Activating the Viral Loop \(Months 7–12\)

- __Objective:__ Push past 500,000\+ users by leveraging built\-in creator mechanics\.
- __Tactic:__ Roll out the "Forkable AI" features\. When early influencers share links to their custom AI assistants on platforms like X or TikTok, their audiences are brought directly into the ORVYN ecosystem to use them, creating an organic user\-acquisition funnel\.
